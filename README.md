# Where is my Ferry — DSS Port Blair

Live ferry status for Port Blair harbour, worked out from the DSS timetable.

- **Where are you going?** Pick Bambooflat, Phoenix Bay, Hopetown, Chatham or Dandus Point and see every jetty you can leave from, the next ferry with a countdown, and every sailing today with its live status.
- **Spot your ferry:** every boat (MV PILOBHABI, MV NORTH BAY, MV MUS, MV DUGONG, MV SHER ALI, MV PADAUK and the others) with what it is doing right now.
- **Live running status:** tap a ferry for its whole day as a timeline of jetty calls, with a moving marker and an animated scene: loading, casting off, at sea, arriving and unloading.

Positions come from the timetable and the usual crossing times, not from GPS, so delays from weather or tide do not show.

Everything is in one file, `index.html`. Open it in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main`, root) to get a public link.
